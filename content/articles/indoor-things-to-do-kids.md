From rainy afternoons and cold Indiana winters to hot summer days, this guide features 39 indoor activities for kids ages 0–9 in Zionsville and Northwest Indianapolis, including Whitestown, Carmel, Westfield, Fishers, Brownsburg, Plainfield, Lebanon, and Lafayette. Explore indoor playgrounds, sensory play spaces, sports, music, dance, art, STEM activities, and free library programs, with options for both drop-in visits and scheduled classes.

Age recommendations are based on published guidelines when available. Many locations offer activities for older children, too, making them convenient for families with siblings of different ages.

For more activities that welcome younger children, including pickleball, badminton, batting cages, cat cafés, and Slick City’s indoor slides, see our guide to [indoor things to do for older kids and teens](/articles/indoor-things-to-do-tweens-teens).

### Build It Playground

**Indoor Playground · Recommended ages 1–10**  
**Location:** [1069 3rd Ave. SW, Carmel, IN 46032](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HBeMatYFmfoaFPW1A)

Build It Playground is a construction-themed indoor playground with ride-on excavators, mini excavators, remote-controlled equipment, building tables, and an indoor sand table. Most activities are designed for ages 1–10, and babies under 1 have a soft-play area, a padded section for crawling and climbing. Large excavators and remote-controlled equipment use tokens, with additional tokens available for purchase. Grip socks are required for children, and adults must wear socks. Signed waivers are required, and reservations are recommended during busy periods.

[Visit Build It Playground →](https://www.builditplayground.com/)

### Carter’s Play Place

**Indoor Playground · All ages**  
**Location:** [1021 Kendall Ct., Westfield, IN 46074](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jVNYHQfayTBUh1U27)

Equipment weight limits apply. Most children who play here are ages 1–6.

Carter’s Play Place is an inclusive indoor playground with adaptive equipment, sensory activities, and space for active play. Children can explore a two-story climbing structure, trampoline, zipline, and foam pit. Open play requires no reservation and has no time limit, and adults are admitted free. Some weekday mornings are reserved for children 4 and under. Children wear Carter’s grip socks, which are sold at check-in and can be reused on later visits. Outside snacks are permitted.

**Accessibility & sensory note:** Calmer Carter’s is a less crowded, lower-stimulation session, available by RSVP. Weekday lunch hours are typically quieter, and staff can adjust the sock requirement for sensory needs or braces.

[Visit Carter’s Play Place →](https://cartersplayplace.com/)

### Dig Dig for Kids

**Indoor Playground · Recommended ages 2–10**  
**Location:** [12800 Ford Dr., Fishers, IN 46038](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KqiabhYtn5xc3ytV6)

Dig Dig for Kids features a giant indoor sandbox where children can dig, build, and play with toy construction vehicles, excavators, shovels, and buckets. Parents can join their children or watch from the sidelines. The Dino Zone, for ages 3 and older, has tables of moldable kinetic sand and custom shapers for creating bricks, dinosaurs, and construction vehicles. The Dino Zone is included with admission, subject to availability. Play is charged by the hour, adults and non-playing infants enter free, and reservations are recommended. A signed waiver is required.

[Visit Dig Dig for Kids →](https://digdigforkids.com/)

### Monon Community Center

**Indoor Playground & Pool · All ages**  
**Location:** [1235 Central Park Dr. E., Carmel, IN 46032](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fpty4wAvW7GqvaST9)

The Monon Community Center has two indoor recreation areas for families. **Monon Station** is an indoor playground with a large climbing structure, slides, a giant light-up peg wall, oversized building blocks, and air-powered tubes that show how air pressure moves balls and scarves. ### Prana Play

**Sensory Playground · Recommended ages 3–12**  
**Location:** [1019 3rd Ave. SW, Carmel, IN 46032](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dB9zXGJrDPoVMQxy9)

Prana Play is an indoor play space designed to be sensory-friendly for every child, including neurodivergent children and children with sensory sensitivities. It has a climbing structure with tunnels, slides, and swings, along with pretend-play settings such as a vet clinic and a grocery store. An open-play pass covers the full day. Classes include Zumbini, a music and movement class for children 5 and under with a caregiver, along with pottery painting and slime-making, and camps are also offered.

**Accessibility & sensory note:** Noise, lighting, and crowd levels are managed throughout the space, and a low-stimulation room is available for breaks. Staff are trained in sensory needs, and the facility offers group occupational therapy, which helps children build everyday motor and sensory skills, along with social-skills programs.

[Visit Prana Play →](https://pranaplaycarmel.com/)

### We Rock the Spectrum

**Sensory Gym · Ages 0 through young adults**  
**Location:** [13644 N. Meridian St., Carmel, IN 46032](https://maps.app.goo.gl/J2gqe576LVqeEJKMA)

Equipment weight limits apply.

We Rock the Spectrum is an indoor sensory gym for all children, both neurodivergent and neurotypical. The main playroom has a padded floor, suspended swings, a trampoline, a large slide, crash mats, and a zip slider, which is a low, padded zip line. Younger children have their own area with tunnels, slides, and toys, and a pretend-play area has dress-up clothes, a kitchen set, and a train track. A padded music room has pianos and drums, and an arts and crafts area has tables for coloring and crafts. Open play is offered throughout the week, and booking online shortens the wait. The gym also hosts classes in a private room.

**Accessibility & sensory note:** The gym was built with equipment for children with sensory processing needs, and a calming room provides a quieter space. Monthly sessions are set aside for infants and toddlers and for young adults.

[Visit We Rock the Spectrum →](https://werockthespectrumcarmel.com/)

## Museums & Family Attractions

These destinations include museums with hands-on exhibits, an indoor amusement park, bowling, and indoor climbing.

### The Children’s Museum of Indianapolis

**Museum · All ages**  
**Location:** [3000 N. Meridian St., Indianapolis, IN 46208](https://maps.app.goo.gl/i14otGWYiM2UiJVi8)

The Children’s Museum of Indianapolis has five floors of interactive exhibits covering dinosaurs, science, history, art, and imaginative play. In Dinosphere, children can see real dinosaur fossils and meet paleontologists working in a fossil lab, while Beyond Spaceship Earth recreates part of the International Space Station. ScienceWorks features a water table, cave, farm activities, and a Discovery Lab for children 5 and under. Other attractions include archaeological digs in Treasures of the Earth, a pretend Peruvian marketplace, and a historic carousel with a mirror maze and playhouses.

Playscape, a gallery exclusively for children 5 and under, has water and sand tables, art and music studios, a climbing structure, and Babyscape for infants and toddlers. Playscape is closed through Nov. 6, 2026, and reopens Nov. 7. Family art activities and workshops give parents and children more opportunities for hands-on creativity.

**Accessibility & sensory note:** Museum My Way events offer limited-capacity visits for guests with disabilities or sensory sensitivities, with a quiet area, reduced music, and adjusted lighting. Registration is required.

[Visit The Children’s Museum →](https://www.childrensmuseum.org/)

### Hoosier Heights

**Climbing · Recommended ages 4 through adult**  
**Location:** [9850 Mayflower Park Dr., Carmel, IN 46032](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PVVpNM6XfbemnDBN7)

Climbers of all ages are welcome, and children 5 and under climb free.

Hoosier Heights is an indoor climbing gym with bouldering, auto-belay, and top-rope climbing. Bouldering walls are short enough to climb without ropes. Auto-belays are devices that hold the rope and lower the climber gently back to the ground. In top-rope climbing, a partner on the ground manages the rope. Adults who want to belay their child on the top-rope walls can take a free lesson that includes a short video and hands-on practice. The gym uses only Gri-Gri belay devices, which have a built-in brake.

Walk-ins are welcome, and first-time visitors complete a safety orientation that includes an introductory video. Rental equipment is included with a first-time pass, including for children 5 and under. On return visits, children 5 and under still climb free but must bring climbing shoes or rent them for a fee.

Two youth programs give kids structured climbing time with counselors. Sunrise Senders is for ages 4–6. The Youth Climbing Club is for ages 7–14 and includes tips, feedback, and climbing games. Both programs require advance sign-up and include gear, and members pay a lower rate.

[Visit Hoosier Heights →](https://hoosierheightsindianapolis.com/)

## Libraries

Public libraries offer free storytimes, play areas, and children’s programs. Access details for nonresidents are noted where the library publishes them.

### Hussey-Mayfield Memorial Public Library

**Library · All ages**  
**Locations:**  
[250 N. 5th St., Zionsville, IN 46077](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Z85WzJeNGRQU135N8)  
[6310 Albert S. White Dr., Whitestown, IN 46075](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nr1pW81eXFz1J7zGA)

Hussey-Mayfield Memorial Public Library’s children’s play spaces include a train table, model train, play kitchen, puppet theater, puzzles, blocks, and sensory toys. Storytimes at both the Zionsville and Whitestown branches include songs and movement with parents, and children’s craft programs are offered. The Library of Things includes STEM Discovery Kits with Snap Circuits and coding robots. The Zionsville branch houses the Zionsville Nature Center, with live turtles, snakes, and amphibians.

**Accessibility & sensory note:** Sensory bags with a weighted blanket, noise-canceling headphones, and fidgets are available during visits. The Library of Things includes Special Olympics Young Athletes backpacks with activities for children ages 2–7 of all abilities, including those with diverse learning needs.

[Visit Hussey-Mayfield Memorial Public Library →](https://hmmpl.org/kids/)

### Brownsburg Public Library

**Library · All ages**  
**Location:** [450 S. Jefferson St., Brownsburg, IN 46112](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RPXHRPav7pRZwLYr5)

Brownsburg Public Library offers storytimes grouped by age, from baby and toddler sessions where parents join in songs and rhymes to preschool storytimes held in the library’s Preschool Room.
