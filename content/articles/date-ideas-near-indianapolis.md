## January: Enjoy a Candlelight Concert in a Historic Indianapolis Cathedral {#candlelight-concert}

**Location:** Scottish Rite Cathedral · [650 N. Meridian St., Indianapolis, IN 46204](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DkGL9wq3LY1V69FZ6)

Fever’s Candlelight concerts take place at two historic Indianapolis venues: Scottish Rite Cathedral and Indiana Landmarks Center. Surrounded by hundreds of glowing candles, musicians perform instrumental arrangements of classical music, film scores, and popular songs. Both venues feature impressive architecture that adds to the atmosphere of these intimate concerts.

For January 2027, *The Best of Hans Zimmer* at Scottish Rite Cathedral features a string quartet performing music from films including *Interstellar*, *Inception*, *Gladiator*, and *The Lion King*. The combination of familiar film scores, live strings, and the cathedral’s Gothic-inspired architecture makes this a distinctive evening out.

**2027 Date:** January 21 at 6:30 p.m. Other Candlelight programs are also scheduled throughout January. Tickets must be purchased in advance.

**Pro Tip:** Seating within each ticket zone is first-come, first-served. Doors open 45 minutes before the concert, so arriving early gives you more options. Some aisle seats provide extra legroom because there is no seat directly in front of them. Free parking is available in the cathedral’s adjacent lot, accessible from North Street.

[Visit Candlelight Concerts →](https://feverup.com/en/indianapolis/candlelight)

## February: Take a Glassblowing Workshop {#glassblowing-workshop}

**Location:** Glass Arts Indiana · [1125 E. Brookside Ave., Ste. C25, Indianapolis, IN 46202](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ixiTbSg4wYafgNQq5)

Create a piece of glass art together at Glass Arts Indiana, a nonprofit studio in Indianapolis’s Factory Arts District. Introductory workshops offer hands-on experience working with molten glass under the guidance of experienced instructors. Depending on the class, participants may gather glass from a furnace, add color, and shape it into a decorative object such as a heart or paperweight. Watching the glowing glass transform as it is shaped is part of the experience.

Glass Arts Indiana offers scheduled workshops and couples lessons, with safety instruction provided before participants begin working with the glass. Finished pieces need time to cool and are typically available for pickup on a later day.

[Visit Glass Arts Indiana →](http://www.glassartsindiana.org/)

### Something Different: Step Into a Valentine’s Murder Mystery {#valentines-murder-mystery}

**Location:** Urban Apples · [18326 Spring Mill Rd., Westfield, IN 46074](https://maps.app.goo.gl/esTfsjQNhpFXfaUF6)

Urban Apples in Westfield hosts *Til Death Do Us Part*, an interactive murder-mystery dinner set at a fictional wedding reception. Wedding attire is the dress code, with guests encouraged to have fun dressing for the occasion. Formalwear, bridesmaid dresses, tuxedos, and even old wedding dresses are welcome. Admission includes dinner, one drink, and the interactive performance. The event is for adults 21 and older.

## March: Watch *Pirates of the Caribbean* with a Live Orchestra Playing the Score {#pirates-of-the-caribbean-live}

**Location:** Hilbert Circle Theatre · [45 Monument Cir., Indianapolis, IN 46204](https://maps.app.goo.gl/a8aY3LtW7YPZQfSD8)

See *Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl* at Hilbert Circle Theatre in downtown Indianapolis, accompanied by the Indianapolis Symphony Orchestra performing the film’s score live. The complete movie plays on a large screen above the stage while the orchestra performs Klaus Badelt’s music, based on themes by Hans Zimmer, in synchronization with the action.

Familiar scenes come to life with live strings, brass, and percussion, bringing a new dimension to the film’s adventurous score. Hilbert Circle Theatre’s historic auditorium provides an impressive setting, with the orchestra visible beneath the screen throughout the performance.

**2027 Dates:** Saturday, March 20, at 7 p.m., and Sunday, March 21, at 2 p.m.

**Pro Tip:** Coffee, cocktails, wine, and other beverages are available for purchase at refreshment areas on both floors of Hilbert Circle Theatre. The upstairs bar is convenient for guests seated in the mezzanine, and concessions are cashless.

[Visit Hilbert Circle Theatre →](https://www.indianapolissymphony.org/about/hilbert-circle-theatre)

## May: Experience the Indianapolis Motor Speedway During Indy 500 Month {#indianapolis-motor-speedway}

**Location:** Indianapolis Motor Speedway Museum · [4750 W. 16th St., Indianapolis, IN 46222](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qv74Ubgx6Xb3w4Fz9)

May brings a different level of activity to the Indianapolis Motor Speedway as teams prepare for the Indianapolis 500. Whether you’re a longtime racing fan or simply interested in Indianapolis history, race month offers a chance to explore one of the city’s best-known landmarks. The renovated Indianapolis Motor Speedway Museum features historic race cars, racing memorabilia, and interactive exhibits examining the people, engineering, and events that have shaped the Speedway.

For a different outing, consider attending an Indy 500 practice or qualifying session, when cars are on the track and visitors can watch the teams and drivers prepare for race day.

## June: Play Pinball, Duckpin Bowling, and Other Retro Games {#pinball-duckpin-bowling}

**Locations:**  
Pins Mechanical Co. · [856 Carrollton Ave., Indianapolis, IN 46202](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Zfpa2KvuBCrD6iRa6)  
The Garage Food Hall · [906 Carrollton Ave., Indianapolis, IN 46202](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Cb39BDHAse7nCVsP8)

Pins Mechanical Co. in the Bottleworks District combines old-fashioned games with a spacious indoor social setting. Couples can challenge each other at duckpin bowling, pinball, foosball, bocce, Patio Pong, Hookie, and Giant Jenga. Duckpin bowling uses smaller balls and shorter pins than traditional bowling, with three throws per frame, making it an entertaining change even for experienced bowlers.

Be sure to explore the second floor, accessible by elevator. Change machines are available for quarters to use in the pinball machines, and guests can enjoy the games without ordering alcoholic beverages. Across the street, The Garage Food Hall offers a variety of independent food vendors for dinner before or after visiting Pins Mechanical.

**Pro Tip:** Duckpin lanes are first-come, first-served, with sign-ups and a waitlist at The Cage near the entrance. Saturday waits can be long, but there are plenty of other games to enjoy while waiting for a lane.

[Visit Pins Mechanical Co. →](https://www.pinsbar.com/locations/indianapolis)

### Something Different: Create a Custom-Scented Candle {#custom-scented-candle}

**Location:** Penn & Beech Candle Co. · [820 Massachusetts Ave., Ste. 1320, Indianapolis, IN 46204](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YK39iN8DYZMbuMju7)

Create your own fragrance at Penn & Beech Candle Co. in the Bottleworks District. At the shop’s fragrance bar, visitors can sample more than 120 scents and choose a combination for a personalized candle. ### Something Different: Take a Behind-the-Scenes Tour of Sun King Brewery {#sun-king-brewery-tour}

**Location:** Sun King Brewery · [135 N. College Ave., Indianapolis, IN 46202](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AGSHXKvuaBG8LT7N6)

Tours are generally offered Saturdays at 1 and 3 p.m. Participants must be 21 or older and wear closed-toe shoes. Tours can be booked online, and food from Nacho by La Margarita is also available at the downtown brewery.

Beyond the monthly date ideas, these seasonal activities offer more ways to spend an afternoon or evening together, including several free or low-cost options.

- **Explore Spring Blooms at Garfield Park Conservatory:** Wander through tropical plants and waterfalls while enjoying seasonal flower displays. Together, the series feature a variety of musical styles, including jazz, country, and classic rock.

- **Listen to Live Music at The Alley Sessions:** The Cabaret in downtown Indianapolis hosts free outdoor concerts in a courtyard-style setting with café tables, colorful public art, and food and drinks available for purchase. [Visit The Alley Sessions →](https://www.thecabaret.org/thealley/)

- **Attend Late Night on Main in Carmel:** On select summer evenings, Main Street in the Carmel Arts & Design District closes to traffic for free outdoor concerts. Bring lawn chairs, enjoy the bands, and explore the surrounding shops and restaurants. [Visit Late Night on Main →](https://www.carmelporchfest.org/events)

- **See Christmas Nights of Lights at the Indiana State Fairgrounds:** Bring coffee, hot chocolate, or cider for a drive through more than two miles of illuminated holiday displays synchronized to music playing through your car radio. Advance online reservations are required. [Visit Christmas Nights of Lights →](https://www.christmasnightsoflights.com/)
