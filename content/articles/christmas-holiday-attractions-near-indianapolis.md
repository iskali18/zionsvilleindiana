## Holiday Lights, Festivals & Displays {#holiday-lights-festivals-displays}

### Winterlights at Newfields {#winterlights}

- **Location:** [4000 Michigan Road, Indianapolis, IN 46208](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MBHZeomG9jDX8qwn9)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 20 – Sun, Jan. 3

Winterlights illuminates The Garden at Newfields with millions of lights along an outdoor walking route. Featured displays include moonGARDEN, a shadow theater show, and *Playtime in Indy*, a tree sculpture covered in toys. Every ticket includes the first floor of Lilly House, decorated for the season, and visitors can stop at Tinsel Terrace for food and drinks and at the Wintermarket for holiday gifts. Advance timed tickets are required. Children 5 and under are admitted free.

[Visit the Winterlights website →](https://discovernewfields.org/winterlights)

### Christmas at the Zoo {#christmas-at-the-zoo}

- **Location:** [1200 W. Washington St., Indianapolis, IN 46222](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dQCsjEmYmFntP1ES6)
- **Dates:** Sat, Nov. 21 – Sun, Jan. 3

Christmas at the Zoo lights up the Indianapolis Zoo on scheduled evenings throughout the holidays. Families can visit Santa, Mrs. Claus, and the elves at Santa’s Magic Cottage and see animals including tigers, penguins, sea lions, and Santa’s reindeer. The event is included with Zoo admission. Children under 2 are admitted free.

Two sensory-friendly evenings, on Wednesdays, December 2 and 16, offer reduced sound, smells, and lighting, with free sensory bags and quiet rooms.

**Pro Tip:** The line for Santa’s Magic Cottage closes one hour before the Zoo closes.

[Visit the Christmas at the Zoo website →](https://www.indianapoliszoo.com/zoo-events/christmas-at-the-zoo/)

### A Merry Prairie Holiday at Conner Prairie {#conner-prairie}

- **Location:** [13400 Allisonville Road, Fishers, IN 46038](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ajbo8iqMmMWcbrQR6)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 27 – Mon, Dec. 28 · Select dates

A Merry Prairie Holiday transforms Conner Prairie’s historic grounds with thousands of Christmas lights, illuminated displays, and seasonal activities. Visitors can explore 19th-century holiday traditions in Prairietown, watch a 3D projection show of *’Twas the Night Before Christmas* on the William Conner House, ride Kringle’s Carousel, slide down the 150-foot Polar Bear Plunge tubing hill, and meet Santa in his Sleigh Barn, where families can take their own photos. Other attractions include Frosty’s Sock Skating Rink, the Merry Marketplace, seasonal food, and outdoor fire pits. The Sleigh Barn is open through Tuesday, December 22, and the rest of the event continues through Monday, December 28. Admission is ticketed. Children under 2 are admitted free.

**Pro Tip:** Bring socks for Frosty’s Sock Skating Rink. Socks are not provided, and skates aren’t needed.

[Visit the Conner Prairie website →](https://www.connerprairie.org/)

### Celebration Crossing at the Indiana State Museum {#celebration-crossing}

- **Location:** [650 W. Washington St., Indianapolis, IN 46204](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kD6TJBKosTCn8HaP6)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 27 – Sun, Jan. 3

Celebration Crossing is the Indiana State Museum’s annual holiday event. Visitors can meet Santa in his house, see the original Santa Claus Express train and the animated window displays from the former L.S. Children can play in Santa’s Front Yard and Reindeer Barn, and visitors can enjoy holiday performances. Celebration Crossing is included with museum admission, and breakfasts with Santa require separate tickets. Children 2 and under are admitted free.

**Pro Tip:** Santa is available through Thursday, December 24, although Celebration Crossing continues through Sunday, January 3.

### Conservatory Crossing at Garfield Park Conservatory {#conservatory-crossing}

- **Location:** [2505 Conservatory Drive, Indianapolis, IN 46203](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hfyyPxafrUVtwNPKA)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 27 – Sun, Dec. 27 · Tuesdays–Sundays · Closed Tue, Dec. 22 – Fri, Dec. 25

## Christmas at Local Farms {#christmas-on-the-farm}

### Christmas Experience at Dull’s Tree Farm {#dulls-tree-farm}

- **Location:** [1765 W. Blubaugh Avenue, Thorntown, IN 46071](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jL4Yf4kUJZwd9kCr9)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 27 – Sun, Dec. 20

Dull’s Tree Farm in Thorntown offers Christmas tree shopping and seasonal activities at its historic 19th-century farmstead. Families can choose and cut their own tree or buy a pre-cut tree, ride wagons through the property, meet live reindeer, and visit a live Nativity display on Saturday and Sunday, December 12 and 13. The farm also has a wreath barn, holiday food and drinks, and outdoor attractions including a barrel train, slides, and farm animals. Mrs. Claus and the Grinch visit on select dates. General admission is free, and some activities have separate charges.

**Pro Tip:** Visitors buying a choose-and-cut tree from Friday, November 27, to Sunday, November 29, must reserve a wagon ride in advance.

## Christmas Markets {#christmas-markets}

### Carmel Christkindlmarkt {#carmel-christkindlmarkt}

- **Location:** [Carter Green, Carmel, IN 46032](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Vpmr8oaWF2oGTu8Z7)
- **Dates:** Sat, Nov. 21 – Thu, Dec. 24 · Select dates

The Carmel Christkindlmarkt is a free German-style holiday market featuring artisan vendors, German food and drinks, seasonal entertainment, and children’s crafts.

Families can visit Santa at the Spielhaus, a children’s entertainment hut with holiday characters and activities. Santa offers free photos on Sundays from November 22 through December 20, from noon to 6 p.m. On Saturday, December 5, visitors can also meet St. Nikolaus (St. Nicholas) during a traditional German Christmas celebration.

Three additional attractions are nearby. The Ice at Carter Green offers outdoor skating for a fee, while the free Magnificent Monon features holiday lights along the Monon Greenway. Palladiscope, also free, projects a 12-minute holiday light show onto the Palladium every half hour in the evening.

[Visit the Carmel Christkindlmarkt website →](https://www.carmelchristkindlmarkt.com/)

## Live Performances {#live-performances}

### AES Indiana Yuletide Celebration at Hilbert Circle Theatre {#yuletide-celebration}

- **Location:** [45 Monument Circle, Indianapolis, IN 46204](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZLL7w3W4zinb8qmA8)
- **Dates:** Fri, Dec. 4 – Wed, Dec. 23 · Select dates

The Indianapolis Symphony Orchestra celebrates the 40th anniversary of AES Indiana Yuletide Celebration at Hilbert Circle Theatre. Conducted by Jack Everly and hosted by vocalist Curtis Bannister, the holiday production combines live orchestral music, singers, cirque performances, elaborate costumes, and choreography. Returning favorites include the tap-dancing Santas and *’Twas the Night Before Christmas*. Before the show, visitors can explore the decorated theater lobby, see costumed holiday characters, take photos, and hear live music on the Wurlitzer organ. ## Mail a Letter to Santa {#letters-to-santa}

- **Locations:** Multiple locations
- **Dates:** Mon, Nov. 23 – Tue, Dec. 15 · Varies by location

<figure class="my-8">
  <img src="/images/articles/christmas-attractions-indianapolis-letters-to-santa.webp" alt="Red metal mailbox with white lettering reading “Letters to Santa” and “Merry Christmas” and a red-and-white striped flag raised against a snowy evergreen background." class="w-full h-auto rounded" />
  <figcaption class="text-sm text-stone-600 italic mt-2">
    Local parks and town centers host Santa mailboxes during the holiday season, allowing children to drop off letters and receive replies by mail.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

Several Indianapolis-area communities offer special mailboxes where children can drop off letters to Santa. Families can write their letters at home and deliver them to a participating location. Children can also receive personalized replies from Santa by mail, provided they follow the instructions for their chosen location. The activity is free, with different drop-off dates and mailing requirements in each community.

**2026 Santa Mailbox Locations**

- **Westfield (Fri, Nov. 27 – Sun, Dec. 13):** Grand Junction Plaza, 225 S. Union St.; Fire Station 81, 17001 Ditch Rd.; and Westfield Washington Township, 17400 Westfield Blvd. **For a reply:** Enclose a separate stamped envelope addressed to your child.
- **Brownsburg (Mon, Nov. 23 – Sun, Dec. 13):** Town Hall, 61 N. Green St. **For a reply:** Write your child’s name and mailing address on the letter. No stamp required.
- **Fishers (Wed, Nov. 25 – Fri, Dec. 11):** Municipal Center, 1 Municipal Dr., and The 106 Plaza, 8100 E. 106th St. **For a reply:** Write your child’s mailing address on the letter.
- **Shelbyville (Fri, Dec. 4 – Tue, Dec. 15):** Santa’s Headquarters on Public Square. **For a reply:** Write your child’s mailing address on the letter. Drop off by Sun, Dec. 13 to meet both published reply deadlines.
- **Whitestown (Fri, Nov. 27 – Mon, Dec. 14):** Whitestown residents only. Municipal Complex, 6210 Veterans Dr., and Santa’s Workshop at Main Street Park, 4286 S. Main St. **For a reply:** Enclose a separate stamped envelope addressed to your child.
