For the Holiday Parade, Santa House visits, and other events in town, see [Christmas in Zionsville](/events/christmas-in-zionsville).

## Holiday Lights, Festivals & Displays {#holiday-lights-festivals-displays}

### Winterlights at Newfields {#winterlights}

- **Location:** [4000 Michigan Road, Indianapolis, IN 46208](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=4000%20Michigan%20Road%2C%20Indianapolis%2C%20IN%2046208)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 20 – Sun, Jan. 3

Winterlights turns The Garden at Newfields into an outdoor walking path lined with millions of lights. Featured displays include moonGARDEN, a shadow theater show, and *Playtime in Indy*, a tree sculpture covered in toys. Every ticket includes the first floor of Lilly House, decorated for the season, and visitors can stop at Tinsel Terrace for food and drinks and at the Wintermarket for holiday gifts. Advance timed tickets are required.

**Pro Tip:** Children 5 and under are admitted free.

[Visit the Winterlights website →](https://discovernewfields.org/winterlights)

### Christmas at the Zoo {#christmas-at-the-zoo}

- **Location:** [1200 W. Washington St., Indianapolis, IN 46222](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1200%20W.%20Washington%20St.%2C%20Indianapolis%2C%20IN%2046222)
- **Dates:** Sat, Nov. 21 – Sun, Jan. 3

Christmas at the Zoo fills the Indianapolis Zoo with holiday lights on evenings throughout the season. Families can visit Santa, Mrs. Claus, and the elves at Santa’s Magic Cottage and see animals including tigers, penguins, sea lions, and Santa’s reindeer. The event is included with Zoo admission.

Two sensory-friendly evenings, on Wednesdays, December 2 and 16, offer reduced sound, smells, and lighting, with free sensory bags and quiet rooms.

[Visit the Christmas at the Zoo website →](https://www.indianapoliszoo.com/events/christmas-at-the-zoo/)

### A Merry Prairie Holiday at Conner Prairie {#conner-prairie}

- **Location:** [13400 Allisonville Road, Fishers, IN 46038](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QRxwfJzEDTNuBMoG6)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 27 – Mon, Dec. 28 · Select dates

A Merry Prairie Holiday transforms Conner Prairie’s historic grounds with thousands of Christmas lights, illuminated displays, and seasonal activities. Visitors can explore 19th-century holiday traditions in Prairietown, watch a 3D projection show of *’Twas the Night Before Christmas* on the William Conner House, ride Kringle’s Carousel, slide down the 150-foot Polar Bear Plunge tubing hill, and visit Santa’s Sleigh Barn. Frosty’s Sock Skating Rink, the Merry Marketplace, seasonal food, and outdoor fire pits are also part of the event. The Sleigh Barn is open through December 22, and the rest of the event continues through December 28. Admission is ticketed.

[Visit the Conner Prairie website →](https://www.connerprairie.org/a-merry-prairie-holiday)

### Circle of Lights on Monument Circle {#circle-of-lights}

### Christmas Nights of Lights at the Indiana State Fairgrounds {#christmas-nights-of-lights}

- **Location:** [Indiana State Fairgrounds, Gate 3, 1698 E. 42nd St., Indianapolis, IN 46205](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1698%20E.%2042nd%20St.%2C%20Indianapolis%2C%20IN)

Christmas Nights of Lights is a drive-through light show at the Indiana State Fairgrounds, with a 2-mile route of light displays synchronized to a holiday soundtrack. The show is open nightly, rain or snow. Online reservations for a specific time slot are required.

- **Location:** [450 W. Ohio St., Indianapolis, IN 46202](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=450%20W.%20Ohio%20St.%2C%20Indianapolis%2C%20IN%2046202)

### WinterFaire at The Children’s Museum of Indianapolis {#winterfaire}

- **Location:** [3000 N. Meridian St., Indianapolis, IN 46208](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=3000%20N.%20Meridian%20St.%2C%20Indianapolis%2C%20IN%2046208)
- **Dates:** Sat, Nov. 21 – Mon, Feb. 15

WinterFaire turns part of The Children’s Museum into an indoor winter carnival with games, animal characters, and hands-on activities. The 47-foot WinterSlide in the Sunburst Atrium runs through January 10, and Santa visits through December 24. ### Celebration Crossing at the Indiana State Museum {#celebration-crossing}

- **Location:** [650 W. Washington St., Indianapolis, IN 46204](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=650%20W.%20Washington%20St.%2C%20Indianapolis%2C%20IN%2046204)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 27 – Sun, Jan. 3

Celebration Crossing is the Indiana State Museum’s annual holiday event. Visitors can meet Santa in his house, see the original Santa Claus Express train and the animated window displays from the former L.S. Ayres department store, and ride the Snowfall Express, a trackless train ride through a snowy forest. Children can play in Santa’s Front Yard and Reindeer Barn, and the season also includes holiday performances. Celebration Crossing is included with museum admission, and breakfasts with Santa require separate tickets.

[Visit the Celebration Crossing website →](https://www.indianamuseum.org/celebration-crossing/)

### Jingle Rails at the Eiteljorg Museum {#jingle-rails}

- **Location:** [500 W. Washington St., Indianapolis, IN 46204](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=500%20W.%20Washington%20St.%2C%20Indianapolis%2C%20IN%2046204)
- **Dates:** Sat, Nov. 21 – Mon, Jan. 18

Jingle Rails is a large holiday model railroad at the Eiteljorg Museum, with G-scale electric trains running on more than 1,200 feet of track through scenery built from natural materials such as bark, pinecones, and acorns. The trains pass Indianapolis landmarks, including Monument Circle and Lucas Oil Stadium, before heading west to national parks, Hoover Dam, Las Vegas, and the Golden Gate Bridge. Jingle Rails is included with museum admission.

[Visit the Eiteljorg Museum website →](https://eiteljorg.org/)

### Christmas Experience at Dull’s Tree Farm {#dulls-tree-farm}

- **Location:** [1765 W. Blubaugh Avenue, Thorntown, IN 46071](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jL4Yf4kUJZwd9kCr9)
- **Dates:** Fri, Nov. 27 – Sun, Dec. 20

Dull’s Tree Farm in Thorntown welcomes visitors to its historic 1800s farmstead for Christmas tree shopping and seasonal activities. Families can choose and cut their own tree or buy a pre-cut tree, ride wagons through the property, meet live reindeer, and visit a live Nativity display on December 12 and 13. The farm also has a wreath barn, holiday food and drinks, and outdoor attractions including a barrel train, slides, and farm animals. Mrs. Claus and the Grinch visit on select dates. General admission is free, and some activities have separate charges.

**Pro Tip:** Visitors buying a choose-and-cut tree from November 27 to 29 must reserve a wagon ride in advance.

## Train Rides {#holiday-trains}

### Holiday Train Rides on the Nickel Plate Express {#nickel-plate-express}

- **Location:** [Hobbs Station, 825 Forest Park Dr., Noblesville, IN 46060](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=825%20Forest%20Park%20Dr.%2C%20Noblesville%2C%20IN%2046060)
- **Dates:** Sat, Nov. 14 – Sat, Jan. 2 · Select dates

The Nickel Plate Express runs several holiday rides from Hobbs Station in Noblesville’s Forest Park. The **Reindeer Express** is a family train ride with holiday music, treats, and a visit from Santa, and the **Holiday Lights Railbike Adventure** is a pedal-powered ride past holiday light displays. Rides for adults 21 and older include **Holiday Cheers**, with an onboard bar; **Holiday Trivia**; the **Ho Ho Homicide Express** murder-mystery dinner; and a **New Year’s Eve** murder mystery. All rides are ticketed and booked online.

[Visit the Nickel Plate Express website →](https://nickelplateexpress.com/train-rides/)

## Christmas Markets {#christmas-markets}

### Carmel Christkindlmarkt {#carmel-christkindlmarkt}

- **Location:** [Carter Green, Carmel, IN 46032](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Carter%20Green%2C%20Carmel%2C%20IN%2046032)
- **Dates:** Sat, Nov. 21 – Thu, Dec. 24 · Select dates

The Carmel Christkindlmarkt is a free German-style holiday market on Carter Green, with artisan vendors, German food and drinks, seasonal entertainment, and a children’s area where kids can do crafts and meet St. Nikolaus. The Ice at Carter Green skating rink next to the market charges separately for skating. Two free attractions are also nearby: the Magnificent Monon, which lines the Monon Greenway through downtown Carmel with holiday lights and decorations, and Palladiscope, a 12-minute holiday light show projected onto the Palladium every half hour in the evening.

[Visit the Carmel Christkindlmarkt website →](https://www.carmelchristkindlmarkt.com/)
