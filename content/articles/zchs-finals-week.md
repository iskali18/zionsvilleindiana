Zionsville Community High School (ZCHS) will begin first-semester final assessments during the last class period on Tuesday, December 15, 2026. Finals will continue Wednesday, December 16, through Friday, December 18, with students completing two finals each morning from 8:30 to 11:40 a.m.

| Date | Assessment blocks |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tue, Dec. 15 | G4 final during the last class period |
| Wed, Dec. 16 | G1 8:30–10:00 AM · G3 10:10–11:40 AM |
| Thu, Dec. 17 | S1 8:30–10:00 AM · S2 10:10–11:40 AM |
| Fri, Dec. 18 | S3 8:30–10:00 AM · S4 10:10–11:40 AM |

**Students will be dismissed at 11:40 a.m.** on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday for independent preparation. A parent phone call to the attendance line is **NOT required** for students to leave at 11:40 a.m. on these days.

**Buses will depart at their regular time of 3:50 p.m.** Families whose students need to remain at school until bus departure may sign them up for on-campus study hall time. Lunch will be available in Eagle Commons at 11:40 a.m. for students staying on campus.

## Dismissal During Final Assessments {#dismissal-during-final-assessments}

To minimize disruptions to the testing environment, students will not be released from class during the finals blocks. It is not recommended to request early dismissal during this time, as students will not be permitted to leave until the final block is over.

## Afternoon Arrangements {#afternoon-arrangements}

**Teacher Office Hours:** Students who need extra help may schedule time to meet with their teachers during afternoon office hours from 12:40 to 3:40 p.m.

**Senior Transition Program:** Seniors in the Senior Transition Program (STP) may continue working in the Media Center or Wedge Commons if they choose to remain on campus during their release periods.

## More Information {#more-information}

For additional information regarding absences and pre-arranged absences during finals, refer to the ParentSquare newsletter **ZCHS Student & Family Update 11-8-26**, published November 8, 2026.
