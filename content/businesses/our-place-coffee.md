Our Place Coffee was a locally owned coffee shop and café at 65 S 1st Street in downtown Zionsville, one block west of Main Street in the Village. It served coffee, tea, breakfast, and lunch in a relaxed setting for conversation, studying, work, or meeting friends.

The café opened in October 2022 and was built as a gathering place for the community. That purpose carried through in the name — a spot where people met, talked, and felt comfortable coming back.

## What it was like

Our Place Coffee had a neighborhood café feel, with indoor seating, outdoor seating in warmer weather, and a location close to Main Street. It drew a mix of people in for coffee, tea, breakfast, lunch, studying, business meetups, and time with friends.

## Food and drinks

The menu covered coffee, espresso drinks, tea, and non-coffee options, along with breakfast and lunch fare. Drinks included brewed coffee, lattes, flat whites, cappuccinos, cold brew, blended drinks, smoothies, refreshers, chai, and matcha, among others.
