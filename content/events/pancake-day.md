The **Zionsville Fire Department** holds its **41st annual Pancake Day** at Station 91. Breakfast is all you can eat, and families can spend the morning with the firefighters who serve the community year-round.

**Admission is $10 for teens and adults, $5 for children ages 2 to 12, and free for children under 2.**

Pancake Day returned in 2024 after a four-year pause, drawing more than 1,200 people and raising over $13,000 for the department. Held at the start of Fire Prevention Month, the breakfast gives families a chance to visit the station and meet firefighters outside an emergency. The menu includes pancakes and sausage with coffee, milk and orange juice.
