Trails lined with hundreds of luminaries, small lanterns lit from inside, create a scenic setting for an evening stroll during Zionsville’s annual Winterfest celebration. Two walks are scheduled at Elm Street Green.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- Both walks are free drop-in events, with no registration required.
- The February walk is a special Valentine’s Day edition.

</div>

## Event Overview

Families, friends, and leashed dogs are welcome at the December Luminary Walk.

The February 14 edition offers a romantic setting for a Valentine’s Day stroll and also welcomes families and friends.
