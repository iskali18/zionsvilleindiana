Horse-drawn carriage rides through Zionsville’s historic Village are part of the annual Christmas in the Village celebration. Scheduled for four December afternoons, the complimentary rides take passengers past historic homes decorated for Christmas, including some of the town’s designated Century Structures.

## Quick Facts

- Free horse-drawn carriage rides are scheduled for December 5–6 and 19–20, 2026.
- Passengers can enjoy free hot cocoa during the ride.
- Advance reservations are available, and walk-up rides are offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Event Overview

The route starts at the Santa House at Cedar and Main Streets and loops along Elm, Walnut, Fifth, and Cedar Streets before returning to Main Street. Brick Street Catering provides complimentary hot cocoa for passengers, adding a seasonal touch to the carriage rides.

## Zionsville’s Century Structures

Established in 1987 by the Zionsville Historical Society, the Century Structure Program recognizes and encourages the preservation of Zionsville’s historic architecture. The program is now administered by SullivanMunce Cultural Center.

To qualify for residential Century Structure designation, a home must be at least 100 years old, receive an Outstanding, Notable, or Contributing rating in the Zionsville Historic Sites & Structures Inventory, and retain the recognizable characteristics of its architectural style.

Designated homes are identified by plaques displaying their approximate year of construction.

Visitors interested in learning more about these historic properties can also explore SullivanMunce Cultural Center’s self-guided Century Structures Walking Tour.
