Movies at Maplelawn is an outdoor fall movie series at Maplelawn Farmstead in Zionsville. Movies are projected directly onto the barn, with guests watching from lawn seating on the farmstead grounds.

The 2026 series takes place on two consecutive Saturdays — September 19 and September 26 — and is presented in collaboration with the Zionsville Cultural District.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- **Gates open at 7:00 PM, and movies begin at dusk.**
- **Bring blankets or lawn chairs for seating.**
- **Pizza, popcorn, desserts, and beverages are available for purchase.**
- **Donations to Maplelawn Farmstead are greatly welcomed.**
- **Check the Maplelawn Farmstead Facebook page for the latest updates and movie announcements.**
