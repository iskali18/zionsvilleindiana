GhostWalk is an annual two-night walking tour through Zionsville's historic Village. During each 45-minute tour, guides lead guests to up to eight stops where actors reenact ghost stories from the town's past. The route and stories change each year.

Tours depart every 15 minutes between 6:00 and 9:00 p.m. on both nights, starting from the front lawn of SullivanMunce Cultural Center at 225 W Hawthorne Street. GhostWalk is presented by SullivanMunce Cultural Center, a nonprofit that operates Zionsville's history museum, genealogy library, and art center.

## 2026 Dates and Times

**Friday, October 2, 2026** — Tours every 15 minutes, 6:00–9:00 p.m.  
**Saturday, October 3, 2026** — Tours every 15 minutes, 6:00–9:00 p.m.

## Tickets

**Tickets go on sale August 1, 2026.**

Pricing after 4:00 p.m. on October 1 and on the days of the event:

- **Adults:** $20 per person
- **Youth (ages 6–17):** $15 per person
- **Children 5 and under:** Free

Advance member and non-member pricing is available before 4:00 p.m. on October 1 through the [SullivanMunce reservations page](https://www.sullivanmunce.org/ghostwalk-reservations.html).

Beginning August 1, reservations can be made online, by phone at 317-873-4900, or in person at SullivanMunce Cultural Center. Payment options include cash, credit card, and check. No physical tickets are issued — reservations are added to a check-in list by last name. Arrive at least 15 minutes before your tour time.

## Plan your visit

**When:** Friday, October 2 and Saturday, October 3, 2026  
**Tours:** Every 15 minutes, 6:00–9:00 p.m.  
**Location:** SullivanMunce Cultural Center, 225 W Hawthorne St, Zionsville  
**Admission:** Ticketed (see pricing above)

**Parking:** SullivanMunce directs GhostWalk attendees to use street parking. See the [downtown Zionsville parking guide](/downtown#parking) for general parking locations in the Main Street area.

## What to bring

Comfortable walking shoes are recommended for the 45-minute outdoor walking tour. Snacks, cider, and water are available for purchase at SullivanMunce during check-in. Tours welcome children, though SullivanMunce recommends an earlier departure time for younger children while it is still light outside.
