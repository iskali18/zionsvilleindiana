GhostWalk is an annual two-night walking tour through Zionsville’s historic Village. During each 45-minute tour, guides lead guests to up to eight stops where actors reenact ghost stories from the town’s past. The route and stories change each year.

Tours depart every 15 minutes from the front lawn of SullivanMunce Cultural Center, the nonprofit that presents GhostWalk and operates Zionsville’s history museum, genealogy library and art center.

## Tickets

**Advance pricing** through 4:00 p.m. on October 1 on the [SullivanMunce reservations page](https://www.sullivanmunce.org/ghostwalk-reservations.html):

- **Member adults:** $15 per person
- **Member youth (ages 6–17):** $10 per person
- **Non-member adults:** $17 per person
- **Non-member youth (ages 6–17):** $13 per person

**After October 1 and on both nights of the event:**

- **Adults:** $20 per person
- **Youth (ages 6–17):** $15 per person

Children 5 years of age and under are free. Reservations can be made online, by phone at 317-873-4900, or in person at SullivanMunce. Payment options include cash, credit card and check. Tickets are sold at the door, but many time slots sell out before the event. No physical tickets are issued — reservations are added to a check-in list by last name. Arrive at least 15 minutes before your tour time.

## Plan your visit

**When:** Friday, October 2 and Saturday, October 3, 2026  
**Tours:** Every 15 minutes, 6:00–9:00 p.m.  
**Location:** SullivanMunce Cultural Center, [225 W Hawthorne St, Zionsville](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JrEjFmiApDDSQeL27)  
**Admission:** Ticketed (see pricing above)

**Parking:** SullivanMunce recommends street parking for GhostWalk. See the [downtown Zionsville parking guide](/downtown#parking) for additional options near Main Street.

## What to bring

Comfortable walking shoes are recommended for the outdoor tour. Snacks, cider and water are available for purchase at SullivanMunce during check-in. Tours welcome children, though SullivanMunce recommends an earlier departure time for younger children while it is still light outside.
