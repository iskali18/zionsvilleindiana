The **Winterfest Open-Air Maker’s Market** is a morning of holiday shopping in downtown Zionsville, just off Main Street, held **Saturday, December 5, 2026, from 9:00 a.m. to noon**.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- Admission is free, and all ages are welcome.
- The market is located in the parking lot at 340 S. Main St., just south of the Friendly Tavern.
- More than 2,200 shoppers attended in 2025.

## What to Expect

Local makers, crafters, and artists sell handmade gifts and specialty goods, with an emphasis on Indiana businesses. Food trucks, seasonal music, and carolers add to the holiday atmosphere.

## Part of Winterfest

The market is part of Zionsville Parks & Recreation’s Winterfest, a seasonal program that also includes [Jingle in the Park](/events/jingle-in-the-park), Lincoln Lights, luminary walks at [Elm Street Green](/events/luminary-walks-elm-street-green) and [Overley-Worman Park](/events/luminary-walk-overley-worman-park), and [Fireside Nights](/events/fireside-nights).

For additional holiday events, see the [Christmas in Zionsville guide](/events/christmas-in-zionsville).

Free public parking is available in lots and on the street throughout downtown Zionsville. See the [downtown parking section](/downtown#parking) for details.

Public restrooms are located in the parking lot where the market is held and are open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., with extended hours during special events. The facilities include separate men’s and women’s restrooms and a wheelchair-accessible restroom with a baby changing table.
