## Event Overview

The Zionsville Show Choirs Holiday Home Tour, previously known as Carol of Homes, opens privately owned Zionsville homes to visitors each December as a fundraiser for the show choir program. Each residence has its own architectural style, interior design, and seasonal decorations.

## Featured Homes for 2026

- **Stonegate:** A traditional Federal-style painted brick home built in 2007, inspired by Boston’s Beacon Hill neighborhood, with family heirlooms and traditional furnishings.
- **Zionsville Village:** A home dating to 1910, combining historic architectural details with renovated living spaces.
- **Holliday Farms:** Two newer residences, one built in 2022 on a wooded lot overlooking the golf course, and a modern traditional home designed for gathering.
- **Ridge Valley Court:** An elaborate Christmas display featuring six trees representing seasonal themes and more than 25 additional trees inspired by Christmas carols.
- **East 300 South:** A château-inspired residence featuring stonework, a spiral staircase, and country views of a pond and creek.

## Tour Booklet and Parking

The official ticket booklet serves as admission and must be presented at each home. Lost booklets are not refundable. The booklet includes homeowner descriptions, each home’s address, and a map of all six properties.

Parking crews will be stationed at all six homes to assist visitors.

## Supporting the Zionsville Show Choirs

Proceeds from the Holiday Home Tour support the Zionsville Show Choirs during the competition season. The program’s two competitive ensembles, the Choralaires and the Royalaires, have earned Grand Champion titles and awards for vocals, choreography, band, and crew at regional and national competitions, including top honors in every category at the Heart of America competition in Orlando, Florida.

The event brings together student performers, participating homeowners, parent volunteers, and local sponsors.
