St. Alphonsus Liguori Catholic Church holds its annual Oktoberfest on Saturday, October 3, 2026, from 4:00 to 11:00 p.m. Open to the public for adults ages 21 and older, the evening brings German food, a Biergarten, live polka music and dancing to Parish Hall and the parish grounds.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- **Oktoberfest runs from 4:00 to 11:00 p.m. and is open to the public for adults ages 21 and older.**
- **There is no cover charge.** Food, drinks and raffle tickets are sold separately.
- **The menu includes German favorites such as currywurst, bratwurst, sauerbraten, spaetzle and Bavarian pretzels.** Beer selections include Sun King Oktoberfest, Cream Ale and Osiris.
- **Proceeds support the Zionsville Food Pantry and The Caring Center in Lebanon.**

## Evening Schedule

| Time | Activity |
| :--- | :--- |
| **4:00–9:00 p.m.** | Oktoberfest dinner |
| **4:00–11:00 p.m.** | Parish Hall and Biergarten |
| **6:00–10:30 p.m.** | Live music and dancing with Polkamotion |
| **9:00 p.m.** | Stein-holding contest and best-dressed prize |
| **10:00 p.m.** | Raffle drawing |

## Food and drinks

Oktoberfest begins at 4:00 p.m. with German food, drinks and seasonal decorations inside Parish Hall and in the Biergarten.

The 2026 menu includes:

- Currywurst, bratwurst, sauerbraten and frankfurters
- German potato salad, potato pancakes, sauerkraut and spaetzle
- Bavarian pretzels
- German chocolate cake
- Apple strudel

Sun King Oktoberfest, Cream Ale and Osiris are on offer, along with wine, apple cider, bottled water and soft drinks.

## Music and dancing

Polkamotion performs from 6:00 to 10:30 p.m. The Indianapolis-area band plays polkas, waltzes and other dance music, with the dance floor open throughout the performance.

A **stein-holding contest** and a prize for the **best Oktoberfest attire** are at 9:00 p.m.

## Raffle

A raffle drawing takes place at 10:00 p.m., and you do not need to be present to win. Parish households receive tickets in the mail, and tickets are also available at the parish office and during Oktoberfest.

## Pumpkinfest earlier in the day

Oktoberfest is the evening half of a single day at St. Alphonsus. [Pumpkinfest](/events/pumpkinfest) runs from noon to 4:00 p.m. and is open to all ages, with free hayrides, pumpkin decorating, face painting, a petting zoo, games and bounce houses. The Oktoberfest menu is available in Parish Hall during those hours, and the Biergarten is open as well.

## About the Event

Oktoberfest is hosted by St. Alphonsus Liguori Catholic Church and the Knights of Columbus.

Proceeds support charities the parish works with year round, including the **Zionsville Food Pantry** and **The Caring Center** in Lebanon.

Visit the [official Pumpkinfest and Oktoberfest page](https://zionsvillecatholic.com/oktoberfest) for event information.
