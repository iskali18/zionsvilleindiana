**Smashin’ Pumpkins: A Pumpkin Smash & Composting Bash** is a post-Halloween event from Zionsville Parks and Recreation where families can bring pumpkins and jack-o’-lanterns to Mulberry Fields Park to smash and compost them. The event also includes family activities, games, food trucks, and composting demonstrations.

The 2026 event takes place **Saturday, November 7, from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM**.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- **Bring pumpkins or jack-o’-lanterns to smash and compost.**
- **Moldy pumpkins are welcome.**
- **Remove paint, glitter, glue, stickers, ribbons, and other non-compostable decorations before bringing your pumpkin.**
- **Limited extra pumpkins are available for a minimum $2 cash donation.** Donations support Zionsville Parks recreation programming.
- **The Epic Pumpkin Drop is one of the main attractions.** Zionsville firefighters raise pumpkins on the ladder truck and drop them from above for a dramatic smash.

## Event Highlights

- **Smashin’ Station:** The designated area for smashing pumpkins and jack-o’-lanterns.
- **Epic Pumpkin Drop:** Watch Zionsville firefighters raise pumpkins on the ladder truck and drop them from above for a dramatic smash.
- **Composting Education & Demos:** Learn more about composting through demonstrations during the event.
- **Kid-Friendly Activities:** Additional activities are planned for children and families.
- **Food Trucks & Games:** Food trucks and games are also part of the afternoon.

## What Pumpkins Can You Bring?

Bring pumpkins and jack-o’-lanterns that can be composted. Before arriving, remove craft paint, glitter, glue, stickers, ribbons, and other decorations that cannot be composted.

If a pumpkin has craft paint or wax on it, scrape or cut away that portion of the skin before smashing.
