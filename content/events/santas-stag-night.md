Santa’s Stag Night is an evening of drinks, football, and socializing at Zionsville Underground during the Christmas in the Village celebration. The event takes place alongside [Ladies’ Night](/events/ladies-night) in Downtown Zionsville.

Four Finger Distillery will provide cocktails, while Vino Mobile Bar will serve beer and wine. Football will be shown on the televisions throughout the evening.

## About Zionsville Underground

Tucked beneath Noah Grant’s on Main Street, Zionsville Underground is a private event venue with space for up to 125 guests. Whitewashed brick walls, a decorative coffered ceiling, and a stained-glass window behind the bar give the interior its distinctive character. The venue has six screens and is accessible by elevator.
