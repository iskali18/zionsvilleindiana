## Event Overview

Set to Tchaikovsky’s celebrated score, *The Nutcracker* tells the story of Clara, a young girl whose Christmas Eve takes a magical turn when her nutcracker doll comes to life. After a battle between toy soldiers and mice, Clara journeys through the Kingdom of Snow to the Land of Sweets, where she meets the Sugar Plum Fairy.

## Central Indiana Dance Ensemble {#central-indiana-dance-ensemble}

Central Indiana Dance Ensemble (CIDE) presents its 27th annual production of *The Nutcracker* in 2026, featuring vocal performances by the Indianapolis Children’s Choir.

### Performance Schedule

| Date | Performance Times |
| :--- | :--- |
| Friday, December 11 | 7:30 p.m. |
| Saturday, December 12 | 2:00 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. |
| Sunday, December 13 | 2:00 p.m. |

### Relaxed Performances

The 2:00 p.m. performances on Saturday, December 12, and Sunday, December 13, offer a more flexible theater environment. Audience members may speak quietly and leave their seats as needed during the performance.

### Sugar Plum Fairy Dessert Party

A Sugar Plum Fairy Dessert Party is scheduled for 12:30 p.m. on Saturday, December 12, and Sunday, December 13, before the afternoon performances. Separate tickets are required for the dessert party.

[Central Indiana Dance Ensemble performance information and tickets](https://www.cidedance.org/current-season)

## Metropolitan Youth Ballet {#metropolitan-youth-ballet}

Metropolitan Youth Ballet of Indiana presents its production of *The Nutcracker* the weekend before Christmas.

### Performance Schedule

| Date | Performance Times |
| :--- | :--- |
| Friday, December 18 | 7:30 p.m. |
| Saturday, December 19 | 2:00 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. |
| Sunday, December 20 | 2:00 p.m. |

Each performance lasts approximately two hours, including a 15-minute intermission.

### Holiday Shopping and Activities

Several Nutcracker-themed activities are available in the theater lobby:

- **Sugar Plum Boutique:** Nutcracker figurines, Christmas ornaments, decorated pointe shoes, and ballet-themed gifts.
- **Land of Sweets Shoppe:** Sweet treats available for purchase.
- **Waltz of the Flowers Sale:** Roses available for purchase as gifts for dancers.
- **Photo Station:** Nutcracker-themed photo opportunities before the performance and during intermission.
- **Cast Meet and Greet:** A chance to meet and take photos with costumed dancers following the performance.

Proceeds from lobby sales support Ballet Theatre of Carmel Academy’s dance education and community outreach programs. Cash, credit cards, and Venmo are accepted.

[Metropolitan Youth Ballet performance information and tickets](https://my-ballet.org/nutcracker/)

### Arrival and Parking

The venue opens one hour before each performance, and theater doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Tickets may also be purchased at the venue, subject to availability.

Visitors enter through Door 1 on the south side of Zionsville Community High School. Free parking is available on the same side of the building, accessible from Mulberry Street.
