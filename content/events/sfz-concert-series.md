Since 2017, St. Francis In-The-Fields Episcopal Church has presented the SFORZANDO Concert Series, welcoming accomplished Indiana musicians and touring artists from across the country to perform in the church nave. Programs have ranged from Renaissance motets to 21st-century jazz. Past performers include Chanticleer, Echoing Air, VOCE, Hot Horns, Trio Pastorale, Catacoustic Consort, The Archibald Quartet and Josefien Stoppelenburg.

The 2026–2027 season continues that range with solo saxophone, tango and jazz, classical guitar, Christmas brass music, solo piano and early music.

## Concert details

### Phil Pierick: Chicago Bagatelles Project

**Sunday, September 13, 2026 · 4:00 PM**

Saxophonist Phil Pierick opens the season with the Chicago Bagatelles Project, a program of solo saxophone music ranging from Bach to contemporary works.

### Becky Archibald and Friends: Wango Tzango Sfortzango!

**Sunday, October 11, 2026 · 4:00 PM**

Composer and pianist Becky Archibald performs original violin-and-piano tangos with Indianapolis Symphony Orchestra violinist Bryson Karrer. The program also includes jazz trios performed with a small combo and celebrates Archibald’s album *Tango for Disobedient Beasts*.

### Alec Holcomb: A Panorama of Classical Guitar Music

**Sunday, November 1, 2026 · 4:00 PM**

Classical guitarist Alec Holcomb presents a program spanning familiar works, overlooked selections and less frequently performed pieces from the guitar repertoire. The program includes music adapted from other instruments as well as works composed specifically for guitar.

### The Indiana Brass: 27th Annual Christmas Brass Concert

**Date to be announced**

The Indiana Brass returns for the 27th Annual Christmas Brass Concert, a family holiday program featuring seasonal music.

### Haewon Yang: Femmes de légende

**Sunday, February 21, 2027 · 4:00 PM**

Pianist Haewon Yang presents *Femmes de légende: A Celebration of Four Women Composers*. The solo recital features works by Florence Price, Clara Schumann, Mel Bonis and Fanny Mendelssohn Hensel, with brief introductions to the composers and their music.

### Black Tulip: Tulipmania

**Sunday, April 25, 2027 · 4:00 PM**

Black Tulip closes the season with *Tulipmania: Music, Art, Politics & Society in the Dutch Golden Age*. The program explores the cultural and political setting of the Netherlands during the 17th and early 18th centuries through music by Jacob van Eyck, Constantijn Huygens, Ferrabosco, Johannes Schenck and Buxtehude, along with Dutch folk songs.

## About the SFORZANDO Concert Series

The name *SFORZANDO* comes from an Italian musical term indicating strong emphasis. Its abbreviation, SFZ, is also a nod to St. Francis and Zionsville.
