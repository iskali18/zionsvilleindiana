The 2026 Zionsville State of the Town address takes place on Wednesday, October 28, at The Club at Holliday Farms. The event is open to the public and features Mayor John Stehr discussing the town’s progress, vision, and future priorities. It is presented by the Zionsville Chamber of Commerce in partnership with the Town of Zionsville.

## Program Schedule

The State of the Town program is presented as part of the Zionsville Chamber of Commerce 2026 Business Leadership Series.

- **8:00 AM – 8:30 AM:** Continental breakfast and networking
- **8:30 AM – 9:30 AM:** State of the Town address by Mayor John Stehr

During the presentation, Mayor Stehr will discuss the town’s progress and future priorities.

## What to Expect

The annual State of the Town gives residents and business owners an update on Zionsville and an opportunity to hear directly from town leadership. Recent addresses have covered topics including town finances, development and growth, public safety, parks and infrastructure projects, and long-range planning. Specific topics for the 2026 address have not yet been announced.

## Location & Registration

The event will be held in the clubhouse at The Club at Holliday Farms.

Admission is $15 per person and covers both breakfast and the presentation. Space is capped at 200 attendees. Advance registration must be completed through the Zionsville Chamber of Commerce by October 19, 2026. Refunds will not be issued after September 30, 2026.

**[Register for the 2026 State of the Town](https://business.zionsvillechamber.org/ap/Events/Register/kgFJ6NXIzCVC1)**

Registration begins by entering your email address under “Your Contact Information.” The email field starts the registration process; it is not a general contact form.
