The annual Christmas Crawl is an evening of holiday shopping and dining in Downtown Zionsville. Part of Christmas in the Village, the event is open to adults ages 21 and older.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- An ugly Christmas sweater contest is scheduled for 8:00 p.m.
- Private horse-drawn carriage rides are available for $50 by advance reservation.

</div>

## Event Overview

The 2026 Christmas Crawl features 24 participating locations, including boutiques, art galleries, jewelry stores, specialty food shops, and restaurants. Visitors can enjoy seasonal drinks and holiday specials throughout the evening.

The ugly Christmas sweater contest will take place at participating businesses.

## Christmas Crawl Carriage Rides

Private horse-drawn carriage rides through Zionsville’s historic Village are available during the first two hours of the Christmas Crawl. Each carriage accommodates up to four passengers.

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Time** | 5:00–7:00 p.m. |
| **Location** | Midwest Jewelers & Estate Buyers · [190 S. Main St., Zionsville](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AwMYwRScDeGfbwmy8) |
| **Cost & Reservations** | $50 per carriage · Advance reservations required |
| **Sponsor** | Stock Yards Bank & Trust |

These rides are separate from the complimentary [Vintage Village Carriage Rides](/events/vintage-village-carriage-rides) scheduled on December weekends.
