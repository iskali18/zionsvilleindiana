Ladies’ Night returns to Downtown Zionsville this December for an evening of holiday shopping, dining, and seasonal specials at local businesses. The annual event is part of Christmas in the Village.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- Participating businesses will have special promotions throughout the evening.
- Ticketed private horse-drawn carriage rides are available by advance reservation.
- For the guys, Santa’s Stag Night takes place at Zionsville Underground during the same evening.

## Shopping Passport and Prizes

Ladies’ Night participants can collect passport stamps at local businesses for a chance to win prizes.

- Pick up a passport at any participating business.
- Collect stamps from at least five participating businesses.
- Submit the completed passport at Radiant Skin.

No purchase is necessary to participate. Winners will be notified at a later date.

## Ladies’ Night Carriage Rides

Private horse-drawn carriage rides through historic Downtown Zionsville are scheduled during the first two hours of Ladies’ Night. Each carriage accommodates up to four passengers.

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Time** | 5:00–7:00 p.m. |
| **Location** | Midwest Jewelers & Estate Buyers · [190 S. Main St., Zionsville](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AwMYwRScDeGfbwmy8) |
| **Admission & Reservations** | Ticketed · Advance reservations required |
| **Sponsor** | Stock Yards Bank & Trust |

These ticketed rides are separate from the complimentary [Vintage Village Carriage Rides](/events/vintage-village-carriage-rides) scheduled on December weekends.

**[Reserve a Ladies’ Night carriage ride →](https://www.signupgenius.com/go/9040C48A4AE2C7-67541978-vintage)**

## Santa’s Stag Night

Also scheduled for December 10, Santa’s Stag Night is an evening for men at Zionsville Underground, located beneath Noah Grant’s in the Carter Building. Cocktails, beer, wine, and football viewing are part of the event, which takes place from 5:00 to 9:00 p.m.

See the [Santa’s Stag Night guide](/events/santas-stag-night).
