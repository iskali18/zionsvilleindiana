Tuff Nutterz is a touring inflatable obstacle course coming to Mulberry Fields from Saturday, October 10 through Tuesday, October 13, 2026. The 1,000-foot course has 30 obstacles, including slides, climbing walls, hanging rails and balance balls. The Tuffy Slide is a separate 20-foot-tall slide that’s included with Full Access tickets.

## Tickets

Most tickets are for a specific 2-hour session. Prices include fees.

- **Standard Access:** $21.99 · Obstacle course · Ages 5 and older
- **Full Access:** $28.01 · Obstacle course and Tuffy Slide
- **Toddler Pass:** $32 · Obstacle course for one child under 5 and one parent or caregiver
- **Full Toddler Pass:** $42 · Obstacle course and Tuffy Slide for one child under 5 and one parent or caregiver
- **Standard Family Pack:** $80 for 4 tickets · Extra family member $20
- **Full Access Family Pack:** $104 for 4 tickets · Extra family member $26
- **Ultimate Tuff Pass:** $80 · Full Access to every session in one day for one person

## Before You Go

Check in at the ticket office at least 20 minutes before your session to collect wristbands. Bring your ticket QR code and signed online waivers for every participant. If you arrive late, you can still join, but every session ends at its scheduled time.

- **Grip socks** are preferred.
- **Long sleeves** help prevent friction burns.
- **Bring water** and sunscreen. Glass is not allowed.
- **Adults must stay on site** with children under 16.
- **No alcohol** is allowed on the grounds.

Food trucks may be on site. There are no lockers, but the ticket office staff will hold valuables for free.

## Parking

Parking is available at Mulberry Fields, with additional parking at Zionsville Middle School directly south of the park. When church services are not taking place, additional parking may also be available at Zionsville United Methodist Church across Whitestown Road.

## Weather

Light rain and wind do not automatically cancel sessions, and surfaces can be slippery when wet. For thunderstorms, heavy rain, high winds or other severe weather, the operator may cancel sessions and issue affected ticket holders a gift card to rebook.
