## Event Overview

Jingle in the Park brings Santa, live reindeer, and holiday activities to Lincoln Park on December 4. Organized by Zionsville Parks & Recreation, the two-hour family celebration gives children an opportunity to meet Santa and see real reindeer up close. Families can also enjoy holiday crafts and activities in the heart of downtown Zionsville.

The celebration also marks the opening of Lincoln Lights, the park’s seasonal lighting display sponsored by Hometown Lawns. The display begins at 5:30 p.m. during Jingle in the Park and continues nightly through February 14, 2027, with lights remaining on until just before midnight.

**Registration:** Advance registration opens November 15, 2026, at 8 a.m. The fee is $5 per child for Zionsville residents and $7 for nonresidents, with space limited to 100 participants.
