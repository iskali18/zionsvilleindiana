Town Showdown is a friendly competition between Zionsville and Whitestown, built around the Big-4 Rail Trail that connects the two towns. Residents turn out for their community, and the town that brings the most participants takes home the Big-4 Rail Trail Golden Bike Wheel.

The 2026 event is Saturday, September 19, at Mulberry Fields Park in Zionsville.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- **Town Showdown takes place at Mulberry Fields Park from 12:00 to 2:00 PM.** The Family Fun Ride to the park is optional.
- **The optional route from Town Hall is about 1.25 miles.** The Family Fun Ride leaves at noon and follows the Big-4 Rail Trail to Mulberry Fields Park.
- **The official headcount is at 1:00 PM.** Be at the park by then to count toward your town’s total.
- **You do not need a bicycle to participate in Town Showdown.** Walking, running, scooters, skateboards, and strollers are welcome.
- **Wear your town colors.** Zionsville participants are encouraged to wear green; Whitestown participants wear blue.

## How the competition works

Participants represent their town by showing up at Mulberry Fields Park during the event. Zionsville residents are encouraged to wear green; Whitestown residents wear blue.

The count happens at 1:00 PM, and the town with the most participants wins the Big-4 Rail Trail Golden Bike Wheel. The trophy travels to the winning community and stays there until the next Showdown.

A bike is not required to participate. Attendees can go directly to Mulberry Fields Park or arrive by bike, on foot, with a stroller, skateboard, or scooter.

## Schedule

- **12:00 PM — Family Fun Ride:** Departs Zionsville Town Hall and travels the [Big-4 Rail Trail](/articles/big-4-rail-trail) to [Mulberry Fields Park](/articles/mulberry-fields-park). The ride promotes cycling as a healthy and sustainable way to travel between the two communities.
- **12:00 – 2:00 PM — Gathering at Mulberry Fields Park:** Food trucks, music, family activities, complimentary bike safety checks, and community booths.
- **1:00 PM — Headcount:** Organizers tally participants for each town to determine who wins the Golden Bike Wheel.

## What to expect at the park

Food trucks are on site throughout the event, along with music, family activities, community booths, and complimentary bike safety checks.

## Getting there

Attendees can go directly to [Mulberry Fields Park](/articles/mulberry-fields-park) for the noon to 2:00 PM gathering, or take the Big-4 Rail Trail from Zionsville Town Hall.

The organized Family Fun Ride leaves Zionsville Town Hall, 1100 W Oak Street, at noon. The trail route can also be traveled on foot.

## Event history

The Town Showdown began in May 2024 as a joint effort by the Zionsville and Whitestown parks departments, marking the completed [Big-4 Rail Trail](/articles/big-4-rail-trail) connection between the two towns. In that first year, Zionsville participants started at Zionsville Town Hall and Whitestown participants at the Main Street Big-4 Trailhead, with both groups traveling the trail to Heritage Trail Park.

The gathering moves between the two communities each year:

- **2024:** Inaugural event concluded at Heritage Trail Park *(Winner: Whitestown)*
- **2025:** Held at Panther Park in Whitestown *(Winner: Whitestown)*
- **2026:** Scheduled for Mulberry Fields Park in Zionsville

## Registration

The Town Showdown and the Family Fun Ride are both free for residents and nonresidents. Advance registration is available through the Zionsville Parks & Recreation online portal, which lists the two activities separately — the Town Showdown from 12:00 to 2:00 PM at Mulberry Fields Park, and the Family Fun Ride from 12:00 to 1:00 PM departing Zionsville Town Hall.
