Gallery On & Off Main is a one-day art walk through downtown Zionsville. Local artists and artisans set up inside participating shops and galleries on and just off Main Street, so a walk down the bricks doubles as a gallery visit.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- **Gallery On & Off Main is Saturday, October 24, 2026, from noon to 8:00 p.m.**
- **The event is free to attend** and takes place in shops and galleries on and just off Main Street.
- **Local artists and artisans are featured throughout the Village**, with original artwork and handmade items available to purchase.
- **Visitors can meet the artists** while browsing participating businesses and galleries.

Participating merchants host local artists for the day, putting original work inside businesses that are not usually galleries. Established galleries along the Main Street district take part as well.

Visitors can meet the artists and purchase artwork directly from them.

Hosts include **Village Mercantile**, **Fivethirty Home** and **CV Art and Frame**, alongside organizer Main Street Zionsville.

## Parking

Free public parking lots are located at the northwest corner of **Main Street and Sycamore Street** and the southwest corner of **Main Street and Pine Street**. Street parking is also available throughout the downtown area. See the [downtown parking section](/downtown#parking) for details.

## About the event

Gallery On & Off Main is organized by Main Street Zionsville, with participating merchants and galleries hosting the artists.

Visit the [official Gallery On & Off Main page](https://mainstreetzionsville.org/art-walk) for the list of participating locations.
