The 10th annual **Zionsville Half-Marathon & 5K, presented by OrthoIndy**, returns to downtown Zionsville on Saturday, November 21, 2026. The course uses the brick section of Main Street, the surrounding countryside, and the Zionsville Rail Trail.

Both races start on Elm Street near Pine Street in downtown Zionsville and finish at Lions Park. The Lions Club Building at 115 S. Elm Street serves as race headquarters for packet pickup and the indoor beer garden hosted by Field Brewing Company.

## The Courses

### Half-Marathon (8:00 AM)

The USATF-certified half-marathon course starts in downtown Zionsville and runs along the brick section of Main Street before heading into the countryside outside town. Runners then return via the flat [Zionsville Rail Trail](/articles/big-4-rail-trail) to finish at Lions Park.

The half-marathon course has six aid stations, each serving water and Gatorade. Energy gels are not provided on the course — runners should bring their own if needed. Portable toilets are available along the course at approximately 3.3 miles, 6.25 miles, and 10 miles.

### 5K (8:20 AM)

The 5K starts on Elm Street near Pine Street in downtown Zionsville and follows a route through nearby neighborhoods before returning to Lions Park for the finish.

The 5K course has one aid station, also serving both water and Gatorade.

## After the Race

Field Brewing Company hosts a complimentary indoor beer garden at the Lions Club Building, where finishers can stop by after the race for a beer.

All finishers in both the half-marathon and 5K receive a finisher medal. Long-sleeve race shirts are included for those who pre-register by Thursday, November 12. Shirt sizes may be limited for those who register after that date.

There is no formal awards ceremony. Age-group awards will not be mailed.

## Parking

The **Lions Park lot** is available but fills quickly on race day. Additional parking is available throughout downtown Zionsville.

Use the [downtown parking map](/downtown#parking) to find public lots and street parking, but expect race road closures to affect access.

## Before You Go

- **Watch for race emails.** Pre-race instructions are sent November 16, and registration QR codes are sent November 20. Show the QR code or give your name at packet pickup.
- **Keep a 16-minute-mile pace.** Both the half-marathon and the 5K require a 16-minute-per-mile pace. Runners who fall behind are asked to move to the sidewalk and continue at their own risk, or board the SAG wagon, a support vehicle that carries runners back.
- **Gear check.** Available in the outdoor shelter next to the finish line.
- **Restrooms.** Available inside the Lions Club Building, at the restroom building near the main Lions Park parking lot, and in portable toilets near the Lions Club Building on Elm Street.
- **Packet pickup by someone else.** A friend or family member can pick up your race packet with your permission. No proof of permission is required.
- **Plan for the weather.** Mid- to late November mornings in central Indiana are typically cool. Layer accordingly.
- **Bring your own gels.** Energy gels are not provided on-course.
