Zionsville’s annual Christmas in the Village Parade & Tree Lighting takes place on Saturday, November 28, 2026, the Saturday after Thanksgiving. The parade travels from Eagle Elementary through downtown Zionsville, followed by the town tree lighting at Main and Pine Streets.

## Quick Facts

- **The parade begins at 5:00 PM on Saturday, November 28, 2026.**
- **Santa and Mrs. Claus light the town tree after the parade.**
- **The parade and tree lighting are free to attend.**

- **5:00 PM** — Christmas parade begins at Eagle Elementary.
- **Approximately 6:00 PM** — Parade concludes near Old National Bank on South Main Street.
- **Immediately after the parade** — Santa and Mrs. Claus light the town tree at Main and Pine Streets.
- **6:30–8:00 PM** — [Photos with Santa and Mrs. Claus](/events/selfies-with-santa).

## The Christmas in the Village Season

The parade and tree lighting mark the start of Christmas in the Village, with downtown shopping events, Santa visits, and ticketed events continuing through Christmas Eve. Brick-paved Main Street, holiday lights, and decorated storefronts also make the Village a popular destination for Christmas shopping, photos, and evening walks.

**Where:** Main Street, downtown Zionsville · Tree lighting at [100 W Pine St](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qimXGdpYCoiH8dd37)  
**Admission:** Free  
**Dress warmly:** This is an outdoor evening event in late November. Bring coats, hats, and gloves, especially if you plan to stand along the parade route.

**Parking:**  
Parking near the parade route is in high demand during the event. Arriving early may help you find a space closer to Main Street.

The largest public lot is at the northwest corner of Main Street and Sycamore Street, with access from First Street. The lot at the southwest corner of Main and Pine Streets is closed for the tree lighting.

Additional parking is available on nearby side streets and in public lots throughout the Village, including Lions Park. Many parking areas are within an easy walk of the parade route.

**Restrooms:** Public restrooms are located in the public lot at the northwest corner of Main Street and Sycamore Street, just south of the Friendly Tavern. The facility includes accessible restrooms and a baby changing table.

## Zionsville Christmas Parade Route

The Zionsville Christmas Parade begins at Eagle Elementary, passes the Hussey-Mayfield Memorial Public Library on Fifth Street and continues east toward Main Street. The parade then heads south along Main Street through the Village, ending near Old National Bank.
