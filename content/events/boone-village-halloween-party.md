The annual **Boone Village Halloween Party** invites families to trick-or-treat at participating shops and enjoy activities throughout Boone Village. The Zionsville Community High School Key Club will host a **haunted hallway and face painting**. The Zionsville Fire Department will bring Sparky the Fire Dog, with the Zionsville Police Department, Boone County Sheriff's Department and library bookmobile also participating.

## Costume Contest

The **costume contest** begins at 6 PM, with first-, second- and third-place trophies awarded by age group. The **K.I.D. Award (Kid's Imaginative Design)** recognizes the most imaginative costume.
