## Event Overview

Santa and Mrs. Claus will be available for photos following the Holiday Parade and Tree Lighting on November 28 until 8:00 p.m. Regular visits at the Santa House begin on Sunday, November 29, and continue on weekends through December 20.

The Santa House is located in the small park at the corner of Cedar and Main Streets. Families can bring their own phone or camera for photos, and Christmas sweaters are welcome.

## Vintage Village Carriage Rides

On December 5, 6, 19, and 20, complimentary horse-drawn carriage rides depart from the Santa House between 3:00 and 5:00 p.m. Families can visit Santa in the afternoon and reserve a carriage ride for later in the day.

See the [Vintage Village Carriage Rides guide](/events/vintage-village-carriage-rides).

Public restrooms are located in the public lot at the northwest corner of Main Street and Sycamore Street, just south of the Friendly Tavern, and are open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., with extended hours during special events. The wheelchair-accessible restroom includes a baby changing table.
