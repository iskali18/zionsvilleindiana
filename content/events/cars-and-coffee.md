Cars & Coffee is a recurring automotive gathering at Graham Rahal Performance in Zionsville. The event features a wide range of vehicles, from rare exotics and vintage classics to race cars, motorcycles, and other enthusiast vehicles.

Cars are displayed throughout the outdoor areas around the Graham Rahal Performance campus, with additional vehicles inside the facility. Visitors can walk among the vehicles, stop at RIVET Coffee Bar, and spend time talking with owners and other automotive enthusiasts.

## What to Expect

Cars & Coffee brings vehicles from different eras and areas of the automotive world to the Graham Rahal Performance campus. Recent events have included rare exotics, vintage classics, race cars, motorcycles, and a variety of other enthusiast vehicles.

The displays extend beyond the parking lot, with additional vehicles inside. Visitors can walk through the indoor and outdoor displays, stop at RIVET Coffee Bar, and spend time talking with owners and other automotive enthusiasts.

The event draws a mix of enthusiasts, families, and casual visitors.
