The 2026 McComb Pella Window & Door Home-A-Rama brought the Builders Association of Greater Indianapolis’ annual custom home show to Wild Air in Zionsville for three weekends in September. Visitors could take a self-guided tour through eight show homes featuring custom architecture, interior design, outdoor living spaces and current home technology and design trends.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- **Home-A-Rama ran Thursday through Sunday on three weekends: September 10–13, 17–20 and 24–27**, noon to 8:00 p.m.
- **Eight show homes** from four Central Indiana custom builders were open for self-guided tours.
- **Tickets were sold online only**, by half-hour arrival window, and started at $20.
- **All sales were final.** There were no exchanges or refunds.
- **Every show home had multiple levels**, which BAGI noted was worth considering for visitors with mobility limitations.

## Eight Custom Homes

Four Central Indiana custom builders created the eight homes featured in the 2026 show:

- **Wedgewood Building Company** — Homes #1 and #6
- **Gradison Design-Build** — Homes #2 and #7
- **G & G Custom Homes** — Homes #3 and #8
- **Old Town Design Group** — Homes #4 and #5

Home-A-Rama was designed for visitors to walk through the completed homes at their own pace, whether they were considering building or remodeling or simply wanted to see new ideas in home design. Builders, designers and suppliers involved with the homes were also available during the show.

## Tickets

A general admission ticket covered the in-person home tour at Wild Air and included access to the virtual experience. Tickets were sold online only, in limited numbers per half-hour arrival window.

| Arrival window | Price |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Noon–12:30 through 2:30–3:00 p.m.** | $30 |
| **3:00–3:30 through 4:30–5:00 p.m.** | $25 |
| **5:00–5:30 through 6:00–6:30 p.m.** | $20 |

Visitors had to arrive within the half-hour shown on their ticket, on the date shown, for it to be valid. The last arrival window was **6:00–6:30 p.m.**, which allowed time to tour the homes before the show closed at 8:00 p.m.

All general admission sales were final, with no exchanges or refunds.

Children were welcome and had to remain supervised. Strollers had to remain outside the homes. All of the show homes had multiple levels, which BAGI noted was worth considering for visitors with mobility limitations.

## Special Events

On **Thursday, September 24 from 5:00 to 6:00 p.m.**, Home-A-Rama held **Home Automation & Technology Night**. Builders and their technology partners discussed the systems and technology incorporated into the show homes.

The **People’s Choice Award** was announced **Friday, September 25** before the show opened. The award was determined by attendee voting during the first two weekends. Old Town Design Group won the 2026 award.

## About Wild Air

Home-A-Rama took place in **Saddleridge Estates**, one of six neighborhoods within the 260-acre Wild Air development. The property includes preserved woodlands, green space and 30 acres of walking trails connecting its neighborhoods.

## Virtual Tour

The 2026 Home-A-Rama offered a **$10 Virtual Experience** featuring interactive 3D tours, photography, and behind-the-scenes videos of the show homes. Virtual access was also included with general admission and did not expire.

There were no refunds on virtual tickets.

## About the event

Home-A-Rama is produced by the **Builders Association of Greater Indianapolis**.
