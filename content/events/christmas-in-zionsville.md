Christmas in Zionsville runs from late November through Christmas Eve. The season takes in the Chamber of Commerce’s downtown **Christmas in the Village** program along with holiday concerts and two productions of *The Nutcracker* at the STAR Bank Performing Arts Center, a seasonal art market at SullivanMunce Cultural Center, and Christmas events hosted by local churches.

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg px-6 py-5 my-8">

## Quick Facts

- **The season opens Saturday, November 28** with the Holiday Parade and Tree Lighting, 5:00–6:30 p.m.
- **Santa is at the Santa House on seven dates**, all Saturdays and Sundays, 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.
- **Zionsville Community High School presents orchestra, band and choral holiday concerts** at the STAR Bank Performing Arts Center in early December.
- **Two Nutcracker productions play the STAR Bank PAC** on consecutive December weekends.

## Christmas in the Village

The Zionsville Chamber of Commerce’s Christmas in the Village program begins Thanksgiving weekend and continues through Christmas Eve, with events along Main Street and the surrounding Village streets.

### Selfies with Santa

**Sunday, November 29; Saturdays and Sundays, December 5–6, 12–13, 19–20 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.**

Santa and Mrs. Claus return to the Santa House at the corner of Cedar and Main Streets on seven dates during the season. Families take their own photos using a phone or camera, with Chamber volunteers available to help.

See the Chamber’s [Santa House Selfies page](https://business.zionsvillechamber.org/calendar/Details/christmas-in-the-village-santa-house-selfies-1916478).

### Zionsville Showchoir Holiday Home Tour

**Saturday, December 5, 2026 · Ticketed**

Presented by Zionsville Show Choirs and long known as the Carol of Homes, the event has traditionally featured festively decorated private residences in Zionsville with Zionsville Community High School show choir students performing holiday solos and small-group arrangements inside the homes.

### Winterfest Open-Air Maker’s Market

**Saturday, December 5, 2026 · 9:00 a.m.–noon**

Main Street Zionsville lists this as the Zionsville Parks Winter Market on the 2026 Christmas in the Village schedule. Zionsville Parks & Recreation calls it the Open-Air Maker’s Market and runs it as part of Winterfest, its winter season program. The market takes place on Zionsville’s brick Main Street, with local makers and artists, food trucks, seasonal music, and carolers.

See the Town’s [Winterfest page](https://www.zionsville-in.gov/737/Winterfest).

### Ladies Night

**Thursday, December 10, 2026 · 5:00–9:00 p.m.**

Ladies Night is an evening shopping and dining event hosted by the Zionsville Chamber of Commerce as part of Christmas in the Village. Participating businesses offer Ladies Night specials from 5:00 to 9:00 p.m. Between shopping stops, visitors can pause at local [Downtown Zionsville restaurants](/articles/downtown-zionsville-restaurants).

## Holiday Art, Concerts and Performances

Several of the season’s larger events take place away from Main Street.

### Teeny Tiny Art Market

**Friday, November 20 – Saturday, December 19, 2026 · SullivanMunce Cultural Center**

SullivanMunce Cultural Center’s Teeny Tiny Art Market features original small-format artwork, with pieces measuring 6 by 6 inches or smaller. The market opens the week before Thanksgiving and continues through December 19.

See the [Teeny Tiny Art Market page](https://discoverboonecounty.com/events/event/teeny-tiny-art-market/).

### David Ackerman Christmas Concert

**Sunday, December 6, 2026 · 7:00–9:00 p.m. · Zionsville Presbyterian Church, 4775 W. 116th St.**

## Church Christmas Events

Several Zionsville churches host Christmas programs open to visitors beyond their own congregations. Christmas Eve and Christmas Day service times are tracked separately.

### Women’s Christmas Tea

**Saturday, December 12, 2026 · Zionsville Fellowship Church**

Zionsville Fellowship Church’s annual Women’s Christmas Tea is open to women ages 12 and older, and the church encourages attendees to invite family members, neighbors and coworkers. The gathering includes a warm brunch, Christmas hymns, and personal stories centered on the Christian meaning of Christmas. The event time and registration details have not yet been published.

See [Zionsville Fellowship Church’s women’s ministry page](https://www.zionsvillefellowship.org/ministries/women/).

[↑ Back to schedule](#schedule)

### Back to Bethlehem

**Sunday, December 13, 2026 · 5:30–8:30 p.m. · Zionsville Presbyterian Church, 4775 W. 116th St.**

Back to Bethlehem turns the Christmas story into a walk-through experience. Visitors travel past six theatrical and interactive scenes before reaching a Bethlehem Marketplace with activities and refreshments.

The tour takes about 30 minutes and runs on timed entry, with visitors asked to arrive 10 minutes before their scheduled time. Admission is **$5 per person, to a maximum of $20 per family**, including the marketplace activities and refreshments.

See the [Back to Bethlehem event page](https://www.zpc.org/event/24467474-2026-12-13-back-to-bethlehem/).

## Parking for Downtown Christmas Events

On most days, free public parking is available in lots at the northwest corner of **Main Street and Sycamore Street** and the southwest corner of **Main Street and Pine Street**. Additional parking is available along nearby streets and in public lots throughout the Village. See the [downtown parking section](/downtown#parking) for details.

Main Street closes to vehicles during the Holiday Parade on November 28. Parking near the parade route fills quickly, so plan to arrive early if you want to park nearby.

The STAR Bank Performing Arts Center and area churches are outside the Main Street event area and offer on-site parking. SullivanMunce Cultural Center is also outside the Main Street event area, with street parking nearby.
