The Zionsville Street Dance is an annual outdoor concert and community gathering hosted by the Zionsville Chamber of Commerce on Main Street in downtown Zionsville. The ticketed event features live music, food and drinks on the historic brick street.

<div className="my-6 p-5 bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-400 rounded-r-lg">

**Update:** The 2026 Zionsville Street Dance has been rescheduled for Saturday, September 26, from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM, with gates opening at 5:00 PM. The Chamber describes the rescheduled event as a scaled-down version of the original August 1 event, with a concert at the north end of Main Street, food and beverage vendors, and sponsor booths. Previously purchased tickets remain valid for the new date. See the [official Street Dance page](https://www.zionsvillechamber.org/street-dance/).

</div>

## Quick Facts

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Date & Time** | Saturday, September 26, 2026, 6:00–10:00 PM |
| **Gates Open** | 5:00 PM |
| **Location** | Main Street, downtown Zionsville |
| **Admission** | $20 advance; $25 at the gate for ages 11+; $10 for children 10 and under |
| **Entertainment** | The Janeways, followed by The Flying Toasters |

**Children:** Children must be accompanied by an adult. There will not be a kids’ area at the rescheduled event.

**Pets and outside food:** Organizers ask attendees not to bring pets because of the large crowds. Outside food and beverages are not permitted.  

**Parking:** Main Street is closed for the event, so plan to park nearby and walk into the event area. The largest available public lot is at the northwest corner of Main Street and Sycamore Road, with additional parking at Lions Park and on side streets around the Village. See the [downtown parking section](/downtown#parking) for details.

**Food and drinks:** The announced food vendors include Fillers, My Sugar Pie, Nothing Bundt Cakes, Greek’s Pizzeria, Eatzo, Lumpia Creations, The Scoop, Chick-fil-A, Willy’s Wagyu, and Lobster Dogs. Beverage vendors include Vino Mobile Bar, Heagy Vineyard, Bier Brewery, Klooz Brewz, Traders Brewing Company, Topo, Travelin’ Tom’s Coffee Truck, and Four Finger Distillery.

## Entry Information

Entrance gates open at 5:00 PM for pre-purchased ticket scanning. Bring your ID and ticket QR code for entry. Attendees will receive an event wristband after check-in.

- **North Entrance:** First and Cedar Streets, open from 5:00 PM to 7:30 PM
- **South Entrance:** Main and Oak Streets, open from 5:00 PM to 9:30 PM
- **After 7:30 PM:** Enter through the South Entrance at Main and Oak Streets

## What to expect

The Street Dance turns Main Street into an outdoor concert venue, with the stage at the north end of Main Street. Local talent The Janeways will open the concert, followed by headliner The Flying Toasters.

The rescheduled event includes food vendors and beverages from local breweries, wineries, and distilleries, along with sponsor booths. The Chamber describes this year’s rescheduled Street Dance as a scaled-down version of the event originally planned for August 1.

## Tickets

Street Dance is a ticketed event. Previously purchased tickets remain valid for September 26.

**General admission:**

- $20 in advance through September 25 at 11:59 PM
- $25 at the gate for ages 11 and up
- $10 for children 10 and under

**VIP admission:** VIP tickets are $75 and include general admission when purchased by September 25 at 11:59 PM. Beginning at 5:00 PM on September 26, general-admission ticket holders can purchase a $50 VIP upgrade at the VIP entrance.

VIP access includes a private cash bar at COhatch, indoor restrooms and air conditioning, cocktail tables and seating near the stage, and access to a stanchioned viewing and seating area in front of Radiant Skin and COhatch. Seating is limited.

Complimentary VIP lanyards provided through COhatch or Radiant Skin do not include general admission; lanyard holders still need a general-admission ticket to enter the event.

The Chamber asks ticket buyers not to use Apple Pay because of technical issues with the ticketing system.

## Hosts

The Zionsville Street Dance is hosted by the [Zionsville Chamber of Commerce](https://www.zionsvillechamber.org/street-dance/). The event is the Chamber’s largest annual fundraiser and one of the biggest gatherings of the year in downtown Zionsville.
