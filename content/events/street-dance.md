The Zionsville Street Dance is an annual outdoor concert and community gathering hosted by the Zionsville Chamber of Commerce on Main Street in downtown Zionsville. The ticketed event brings thousands of people to the Village for live music, local drinks, food trucks, and family-friendly activities on the historic brick street.

## What to expect

The Street Dance turns Main Street into an outdoor concert venue. The stage is set up at the north end of Main Street, with music, food, drinks, and event activity spread through the downtown area.

Zionsville’s The Janeways will open the 2026 Street Dance from 6:30 PM to 7:30 PM, followed by headliner The Flying Toasters.

Local breweries, wineries, and distilleries serve drinks throughout the event, and food trucks offer a range of dinner and snack options. Families can also find a kids’ area on-site.

Main Street is closed to vehicles during the event. Pre-purchased tickets are scanned at the gate beginning at 5:00 PM. Previously purchased tickets remain valid for the rescheduled event.

## Plan your visit

**When:** Saturday, September 26, 2026, 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM  
**Location:** Main Street, downtown Zionsville  
**Admission:** Ticketed. Previously purchased tickets remain valid for the rescheduled event. Advance tickets are $20 for ages 11 and up and $10 for children 10 and under.  
**Family:** Kids’ area on-site. Children must be accompanied by an adult.  
**Pets and outside food:** Organizers ask attendees not to bring pets because of the large crowds. Outside food and beverages are not permitted.  

**Parking:** Main Street is closed for the event, so plan to park nearby and walk into the event area. The largest available public lot is at the northwest corner of Main Street and Sycamore Road, with additional parking at Lions Park and on side streets around the Village. Spaces near the event fill up before the 5:00 PM gate opening. See the [downtown parking section](/downtown#parking) for details.

**Food and drinks:** Local breweries, wineries, distilleries, and food trucks are on-site. Most vendors accept cards. ID is checked for alcohol purchases.

## Hosts

The Zionsville Street Dance is hosted by the [Zionsville Chamber of Commerce](https://business.zionsvillechamber.org/calendar/Details/zionsville-street-dance-2026-1893390). The event is the Chamber’s largest annual fundraiser and one of the biggest gatherings of the year in downtown Zionsville.
