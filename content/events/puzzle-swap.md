## Event Overview

The annual Zionsville Puzzle Swap gives jigsaw puzzle enthusiasts an opportunity to exchange completed puzzles for new challenges. Organized by Zionsville Parks & Recreation in celebration of National Puzzle Day, the event brings community members together to share their collections and discover different puzzles. The swap has attracted considerable participation, with more than 1,700 puzzles exchanged in just two hours during the 2026 event.

The 2027 Puzzle Swap takes place Saturday, January 30, at Zionsville Town Hall. General admission is from 9–11 a.m., with a limited early-bird session available before the main event. Participants can browse the collected puzzles and select replacements using tickets received during the January drop-off period.

## How the Puzzle Swap Works

**1. Drop Off Your Puzzles**

Bring complete, gently used puzzles to Zionsville Town Hall between January 4 and 27, during regular business hours of 8 a.m.–4 p.m. Town Hall will be closed Monday, January 18, and puzzles are not accepted on the day of the swap.

**2. Collect Your Exchange Tickets**

Participants receive one ticket for each accepted puzzle, up to a maximum of 10 tickets per person. Keep these tickets for swap day, as they are required for entry and exchanges.

**3. Return on January 30**

Bring your exchange tickets to Town Hall between 9 and 11 a.m. to select replacement puzzles. Each ticket can be exchanged for one puzzle.

## Puzzle Requirements

Zionsville Parks & Recreation accepts puzzles of various sizes, subject to the following requirements:

- Puzzles must be complete, with no missing pieces.
- All puzzles must be in their original boxes or tins.
- Puzzles must be in good condition and no more than 10 years old.
- Wooden puzzles intended for babies or toddlers are excluded.
- Puzzles containing profanity, nudity, or drug- or alcohol-related paraphernalia are excluded.

## Early-Bird Admission

Early-bird registration opens **January 4, 2027, at 8 a.m.** for residents and nonresidents. Only 50 spaces are available.

Early-bird tickets are $10 per person and allow admission from 8–8:45 a.m. on January 30, before the general swap begins.

Every early-bird attendee, including children, must have an early-bird admission ticket. Participants also need the puzzle exchange tickets received during drop-off.

Coffee and hot chocolate are provided during early-bird access. Early-bird participants must complete their exchanges before general admission begins at 9 a.m.
