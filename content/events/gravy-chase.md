The Gravy Chase is a Thanksgiving morning run and walk through historic downtown Zionsville, now in its **10th year**. The Zionsville race follows Main Street and offers a **2.2-mile** and a **4-mile** route, each available as a run or a walk.

It is the largest annual fundraiser for the **Boys & Girls Club of Boone County**, which runs the event at two locations. A separate 5K takes place the same morning in downtown Lebanon.

## Quick Facts

- **The Zionsville race starts at 8:30 a.m. on Thursday, November 26, 2026**, on Main Street near Rosie’s Place.
- **Registration starts at $45** and has four price tiers through race morning. Family pricing is available.
- **Packet pickup is Wednesday, November 25** from noon to 6 p.m. at the Boys & Girls Club Zionsville Unit.
- **Strollers, wheelchairs and dogs are welcome**, but skates, skateboards and bikes are not permitted.
- **All proceeds support the Boys & Girls Club of Boone County**, with a 2026 goal of $200,000.

## The courses

**Zionsville** has two distances, both starting and finishing on Main Street:

- **2.2-mile run or walk** — 8:30 a.m.
- **4-mile run or walk** — 8:30 a.m.

**Lebanon** has a **5K run or walk**, also at 8:30 a.m., starting in downtown Lebanon near Klooz Brewz.

See the [Zionsville course maps](https://runsignup.com/Race/bgcboonegravychase/Page-5) and the [Lebanon course map](https://runsignup.com/Race/bgcboonegravychase/Page-6).

## Registration

Individual registration for the in-person races has four price tiers. A RunSignup processing fee is added at checkout.

| Register by | Price |
| :--- | :--- |
| **September 30** | $45 |
| **October 31** | $50 |
| **November 25** | $55 |
| **Race morning, until 8:29 a.m.** | $60 |

**Family pricing** covers four, five or six registrants and starts at $150 for a family of four. Rates increase by registration period, and family registration closes **November 23**.

An **event shirt on its own** is $25, without race entry, available through **November 25**. Shirt sizes are guaranteed for anyone registered by **November 11**; after that, remaining sizes are first come, first served.

There is also a **bundle with [Hit the Bricks](/events/hit-the-bricks)**, the Zionsville Optimist Club’s October run/walk, at 15 percent off each event.

Race registrations are nonrefundable. Participants who cannot attend the in-person race may transfer to the virtual option.

Sign up on the [Gravy Chase registration page](https://runsignup.com/gravychase).

## Virtual option

A **virtual 5K** is also available for **$45** for participants who prefer to run or walk on their own.

See the [Gravy Chase registration page](https://runsignup.com/gravychase) for current virtual race details and instructions.

## Packet pickup

**Wednesday, November 25, noon to 6 p.m.**

Zionsville racers collect packets at the **Boys & Girls Club of Boone County Zionsville Unit**, and Lebanon racers at the **Lebanon Unit**. Packets must be collected at the location matching your race.

Forgot your bib on race day? The Boys & Girls Club tent near Rush on Main in Zionsville can issue a replacement.

## Prizes and team contests

Prizes go to the **top three male and top three female finishers** in each in-person race.

Teams register in two categories, **Corporate** and **Friends and Family**, with a deadline of **6 p.m. on Wednesday, November 25**. Awards go to the largest team, the most fundraised team, the most fundraised individual, and best costume.

## On race morning

Portable restrooms are in the parking lot southeast of **Pine and Main Street**.

Free public parking lots are at the northwest corner of **Main Street and Sycamore Street** and the southwest corner of **Main Street and Pine Street**. Street parking is also available throughout the downtown area, though expect road closures along the course. See the [downtown parking section](/downtown#parking) for details.

## About the event

The Gravy Chase is organized by the **Boys & Girls Club of Boone County**. All proceeds go to Club programs and services for children across the county, with a 2026 goal of **$200,000**.

Donations can be made at [bgcboone.org](https://bgcboone.org).
